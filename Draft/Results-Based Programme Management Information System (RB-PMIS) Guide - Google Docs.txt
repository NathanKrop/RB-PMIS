​SYSTEM​​LOG GUIDE​
​Development of an Integrated Results-Based Programme Management Information System​
​(RB-PMIS)​
​1. Background and Rationale​
​Effective programme implementation requires more than tracking completed activities; it​
​requires understanding whether interventions are contributing to meaningful changes and​
​achieving intended results. Organizations increasingly require systems that support​
​results-based management by linking strategic objectives, outcomes, outputs, activities,​
​indicators, evidence and performance reporting.​
​Currently, departmental reporting is conducted through Excel-based templates that capture​
​weekly activities completed, key metrics, challenges, actions taken, lessons learned and planned​
​activities. While this approach provides a basic reporting structure, it is largely activity-focused​
​and requires significant manual effort to consolidate information across departments.​
​The current process presents several limitations:​
​●​ ​Limited linkage between departmental work plans and reported achievements​
​●​ ​Focus on activities completed rather than measurable results achieved​
​●​ ​Manual consolidation of multiple departmental reports​
​●​ ​Limited ability to track progress against strategic objectives and indicators​
​●​ ​Difficulty analysing trends, recurring challenges, and programme performance over time​
​●​ ​Limited availability of real-time information for management decision-making​
​To address these challenges, there is a need to develop an Integrated Results-Based Programme​
​Management Information System (RB-PMIS). The system will provide a centralized platform that​
​connects strategic planning, departmental work plans, implementation tracking, outcome​
​monitoring, reporting and organizational learning.​
​The RB-PMIS will enable departments to report not only on what they have done, but also on​
​the results achieved, evidence generated, challenges affecting performance and adaptations​
​required to improve programme effectiveness.​

​2. Purpose of the System​
​The purpose of developing the RB-PMIS is to establish an integrated digital platform that​
​strengthens results-based planning, monitoring, reporting and decision-making across the​
​organization.​
​The system will ensure that departmental activities are directly linked to organizational​
​outcomes and strategic priorities, enabling management to track progress, measure​
​performance and make informed decisions based on reliable programme data.​
​3. Objectives​
​The objectives of the RB-PMIS are to:​
​1.​ ​Establish a centralized digital platform for results-based planning, monitoring and​
​reporting​
​2.​ ​Link departmental work plans with organizational outcomes, outputs, indicators and​
​targets​
​3.​ ​Shift reporting from activity-based updates to outcome-focused performance reporting​
​4.​ ​Improve the quality, consistency and timeliness of programme information​
​5.​ ​Enable real-time tracking of progress against planned results and performance indicators​
​6.​ ​Reduce manual consolidation and preparation of programme reports​
​7.​ ​Strengthen accountability, transparency and evidence-based decision-making​
​8.​ ​Promote organizational learning through systematic documentation of lessons,​
​challenges and adaptive actions.​
​4. Proposed System Framework​
​The RB-PMIS will follow a results chain approach:​
​Strategic Objectives → Outcomes → Outputs → Activities → Indicators → Evidence → Results​
​Reporting​
​Example:​
​Strategic Objective:​
​Improve access to quality services.​
​↓​

​Outcome:​
​Communities have improved access to essential services.​
​↓​
​Outcome Indicators:​
​●​ ​Percentage increase in service utilization.​
​●​ ​Number of beneficiaries accessing services.​
​●​ ​Beneficiary satisfaction levels.​
​↓​
​Outputs:​
​●​ ​Community awareness sessions conducted.​
​●​ ​Service providers strengthened.​
​↓​
​Activities:​
​●​ ​Conduct community engagement sessions.​
​●​ ​Train service providers.​
​●​ ​Develop communication materials.​
​5. Key System Components​
​5.1 Strategic Results Framework Module​
​This module will store and manage the organization's results framework, including:​
​●​ ​Strategic objectives​
​●​ ​Outcomes​
​●​ ​Outputs​
​●​ ​Indicators​
​●​ ​Baselines​
​●​ ​Targets​
​●​ ​Reporting frequencies​
​●​ ​Responsible departments​

​This ensures that all departmental activities contribute to clearly defined organizational results.​
​5.2 Departmental Work Planning Module​
​The Departmental Work Planning Module will provide a centralized platform where​
​departments develop, manage, track and update their annual, quarterly and monthly work​
​plans. The module will ensure that departmental plans are aligned with organizational​
​objectives and provide a foundation for monitoring implementation progress and reporting​
​results.​
​The system will move work planning from a static document-based process into a dynamic​
​planning and performance management tool where planned activities, expected results,​
​progress updates, risks and evidence can be tracked throughout the implementation period.​
​The module will capture the following information:​
​Planning Framework​
​●​ ​Strategic objective or departmental objective​
​●​ ​Quarter and reporting period which will be sequenced as weekly, Monthly and quarterly.​
​●​ ​Specific activity and activity description​
​●​ ​Expected outputs​
​●​ ​Intended outcomes​
​●​ ​Performance indicators​
​●​ ​Targets​
​●​ ​Responsible person​
​●​ ​Implementation timelines​
​●​ ​Required resources​
​Activity Implementation Tracking​
​For each planned activity, the system will allow departments to track:​
​●​ ​Monthly implementation schedule​
​●​ ​Activity status​
​●​ ​Progress updates​
​●​ ​Actual achievements against planned targets​
​●​ ​Variances between planned and actual implementation​

​Results and Indicator Tracking​
​The system will capture measurable indicators of success linked to each planned activity.​
​Indicators will be defined clearly to support results-based monitoring and may include​
​quantitative or qualitative measures.​
​Examples:​
​●​ ​Number of participants reached​
​●​ ​Number of sessions conducted​
​●​ ​Percentage completion of planned activities​
​●​ ​Number of services delivered​
​●​ ​Level of stakeholder engagement achieved​
​Evidence and Means of Verification​
​Each activity will include clearly defined evidence requirements to support verification of​
​reported achievements.​
​The system will allow departments to specify and upload relevant means of verification,​
​including:​
​●​ ​Attendance registers​
​●​ ​Signed participant lists​
​●​ ​Activity reports​
​●​ ​Monitoring checklists​
​●​ ​Survey results​
​●​ ​Assessment reports​
​●​ ​Photographs​
​●​ ​Videos​
​●​ ​Other approved supporting documentation​
​The system will encourage departments to specify the exact evidence required rather than using​
​general descriptions.​
​Resource Planning​
​The module will capture all inputs required for successful implementation, including:​
​●​ ​Human resources​
​●​ ​Financial resources​

​●​ ​Materials and supplies​
​●​ ​Equipment​
​●​ ​Other operational requirements​
​Risk and Mitigation Tracking​
​The system will capture anticipated risks and implementation challenges associated with​
​planned activities, together with corresponding mitigation measures.​
​This will enable proactive monitoring of factors that may affect achievement of planned results.​
​Work Plan Monitoring and Reporting Linkage​
​The work planning module will be directly linked to the reporting module, allowing departments​
​to provide progress updates against approved plans rather than preparing separate activity​
​reports.​
​The linkage will enable the organization to track:​
​●​ ​Planned activities versus completed activities​
​●​ ​Expected results versus actual achievements​
​●​ ​Indicator progress against targets​
​●​ ​Implementation challenges and responses​
​●​ ​Evidence supporting reported achievements​
​This integrated approach will ensure that work planning, implementation monitoring, evidence​
​management and results reporting are connected within a single system.​
​5.3 Results Monitoring and Performance Tracking Module​
​This module will enable departments to track progress against indicators.​
​The system will capture:​
​●​ ​Indicator values​
​●​ ​Baseline information​
​●​ ​Targets​
​●​ ​Current achievement​
​●​ ​Percentage progress​
​●​ ​Supporting evidence​

​5.4 Outcome-Based Reporting Module​
​Weekly and periodic reporting will move from activity reporting to results reporting.​
​Departments will provide updates on:​
​Outcome Progress:​​What changes or results have been​​achieved during the reporting period?​
​Key Results Achieved:​​What measurable improvements​​or outputs were delivered?​
​Evidence:​​What documents or data demonstrate achievement?​
​Examples:​
​●​ ​Attendance records​
​●​ ​Survey results​
​●​ ​Assessment reports​
​●​ ​Monitoring reports​
​●​ ​Photos or verification documents​
​Challenges Affecting Results​
​What factors affected progress toward outcomes?​
​Adaptive Actions​
​What actions were taken to address challenges and improve performance?​
​Lessons Learned​
​What approaches worked, what did not work, and what should be adjusted?​
​Priorities for the Next Reporting Period​
​What results will be prioritized next?​
​5.5 Dashboard and Analytics Module​
​The system will provide role-based dashboards designed to provide users with information​
​relevant to their responsibilities while maintaining appropriate data governance and reporting​
​controls.​

​The dashboard structure will follow a reporting hierarchy where departmental submissions are​
​reviewed and consolidated by the Programme Reporting Officer before summarized information​
​is presented to management.​
​Management Dashboard( Program Manager/ Executive Director)​
​The management dashboard will provide a high-level overview of organizational performance​
​and will focus on strategic decision-making information, including:​
​●​ ​Progress against strategic outcomes​
​●​ ​Achievement of key indicators and targets​
​●​ ​Overall programme performance​
​●​ ​Progress against departmental commitments​
​●​ ​Delayed activities requiring attention​
​●​ ​Emerging risks and implementation barriers​
​●​ ​Common challenges affecting programme delivery​
​●​ ​Key results and achievements​
​●​ ​Areas requiring management decisions or support​
​Management users will access validated and consolidated information prepared through the​
​programme reporting process rather than unreviewed departmental submissions. This will​
​ensure that management decisions are based on accurate, analysed and quality-assured​
​information.​
​Programme Reporting Officer Dashboard​
​The Programme Reporting Officer dashboard will provide more detailed oversight and reporting​
​management functionality, including:​
​●​ ​Departmental submission status​
​●​ ​Pending and overdue reports​
​●​ ​Results achieved by department​
​●​ ​Indicator performance tracking​
​●​ ​Progress against planned activities and outcomes​
​●​ ​Reporting trends over time​
​●​ ​Evidence submission status​
​●​ ​Areas requiring follow-up or clarification​
​●​ ​Data quality issues requiring attention​
​●​ ​Recurring challenges and lessons emerging across departments​

​Departmental Dashboard​
​Departmental users will have access to their own reporting and performance information,​
​including:​
​●​ ​Department work plan progress​
​●​ ​Planned versus completed activities​
​●​ ​Outcome contributions​
​●​ ​Indicator achievement​
​●​ ​Pending reporting requirements​
​●​ ​Uploaded evidence status​
​●​ ​Previous submissions and lessons learned​
​This role-based dashboard approach will ensure that departments are accountable for​
​reporting, the Programme Reporting Officer maintains quality assurance and consolidation​
​responsibilities, and management receives concise, strategic information to support​
​decision-making.​
​5.6 Automated Reporting Module​
​The system will generate:​
​●​ ​Weekly programme updates​
​●​ ​Monthly performance reports​
​●​ ​Quarterly results reports​
​●​ ​Annual reports​
​●​ ​Donor reporting summaries​
​●​ ​Management briefs​
​Reports will be exportable into:​
​●​ ​Word.​
​●​ ​PDF.​
​●​ ​Excel.​
​5.7 Workflow and Notification Module​
​The system will support:​
​●​ ​Reporting deadlines​
​●​ ​Automated reminders​

​●​ ​Approval processes​
​●​ ​Follow-up notifications​
​●​ ​Escalation of overdue submissions​
​5.8 Knowledge Management and Learning Repository​
​The system will store:​
​●​ ​Previous reports​
​●​ ​Lessons learned​
​●​ ​Good practices​
​●​ ​Supporting evidence​
​●​ ​Programme documents​
​This will strengthen institutional learning and knowledge sharing.​
​5.9 Evidence, Document Management and Verification Module​
​A core component of the Integrated Results-Based Programme Management Information​
​System (RB-PMIS) will be an Evidence, Document Management and Verification Module. This​
​module will ensure that reported results, outputs, and indicators are supported by appropriate​
​and verifiable evidence.​
​The module will provide a centralized repository where departments can upload, organize, link,​
​review, and retrieve programme documentation associated with planned activities, achieved​
​results, and performance indicators. This will strengthen accountability, improve data quality,​
​support audit requirements, and enhance confidence in reported programme achievements.​
​Types of Evidence Supported​
​The system will allow departments to upload and manage different forms of programme​
​evidence, including:​
​●​ ​Activity completion reports​
​●​ ​Attendance sheets and participant registers​
​●​ ​Training reports​
​●​ ​Workshop and meeting documentation​
​●​ ​Signed participant lists​
​●​ ​Monitoring and supervision reports​
​●​ ​Assessment and evaluation reports​

​●​ ​Surveys and feedback forms​
​●​ ​Photos and videos from field activities​
​●​ ​Case studies and success stories​
​●​ ​Beneficiary testimonials​
​●​ ​Communication materials​
​●​ ​Presentations​
​●​ ​Signed agreements and approval documents​
​●​ ​Any other relevant supporting documentation required for verification​
​●​ ​Concept Notes​
​Evidence Linking and Classification​
​All uploaded evidence will be linked directly to the relevant programme components to ensure​
​traceability and accountability. Each document will be associated with:​
​●​ ​Strategic objective​
​●​ ​Outcome​
​●​ ​Output​
​●​ ​Activity​
​●​ ​Indicator​
​●​ ​Target​
​●​ ​Reporting period​
​●​ ​Responsible department​
​●​ ​Responsible officer​
​Example:​
​Outcome:​​Improved access to community services​
​Indicator:​​Number of beneficiaries reached​
​Reported Achievement:​​500 beneficiaries reached during​​the reporting period​
​Supporting Evidence:​
​●​ ​Participant attendance registers​
​●​ ​Activity reports​
​●​ ​Field photographs​
​●​ ​Beneficiary feedback forms​
​●​ ​Monitoring checklists​

​Evidence Capture​
​For programmes involving field implementation, the system will support field evidence capture​
​through:​
​●​ ​Photo uploads​
​●​ ​Video uploads​
​●​ ​Mobile-based evidence submission​
​●​ ​Activity-linked photographs​
​●​ ​Descriptions accompanying uploaded evidence​
​This will improve verification of field activities and strengthen the credibility of reported​
​achievements.​
​Example:​
​A field officer uploads a photograph from a community engagement activity. The system​
​records:​
​●​ ​Activity: Community Convening​
​●​ ​Location: Site A​
​●​ ​Date: 15 July 2026​
​●​ ​Uploaded by: Responsible Officer​
​●​ ​Linked Output: Community members reached​
​●​ ​Linked Indicator: Number of participants reached​
​Document Management Features​
​The module will provide:​
​Secure Storage​
​●​ ​Centralized storage of programme documents​
​●​ ​Controlled access based on user roles​
​●​ ​Protection against unauthorized changes​
​Organization and Search​
​Users will be able to search documents by:​
​●​ ​Department​
​●​ ​Programme area​

​●​ ​Outcome​
​●​ ​Indicator​
​●​ ​Reporting period​
​●​ ​Document type​
​●​ ​Keywords​
​Version Control​
​The system will maintain:​
​●​ ​Previous versions of documents​
​●​ ​Date of updates​
​●​ ​Person responsible for changes​
​Evidence Completeness Tracking​
​The system will identify:​
​●​ ​Reports submitted without supporting evidence​
​●​ ​Indicators requiring verification​
​●​ ​Missing documentation​
​●​ ​Pending approvals​
​Evidence Review and Verification Workflow​
​The system will support an internal quality assurance process:​
​Step 1: Department Submission​
​Department leads submit:​
​●​ ​Results update​
​●​ ​Indicator progress​
​●​ ​Supporting evidence​
​Step 2: Review​
​The Programme Reporting Officer reviews:​
​●​ ​Completeness of information​
​●​ ​Accuracy of reported achievements​
​●​ ​Availability of supporting evidence​

​Step 3: Verification​
​Evidence is checked against reported results and assigned a verification status:​
​●​ ​Verified​
​●​ ​Requires clarification​
​●​ ​Pending additional evidence​
​●​ ​Rejected​
​Step 4: Approval and Reporting​
​Verified information is incorporated into:​
​●​ ​Weekly programme reports​
​●​ ​Monthly performance reports​
​●​ ​Quarterly results reports​
​●​ ​Donor reporting documents​

​●​ ​Reduce time spent searching for supporting documents​
​●​ ​Improve audit preparedness​
​●​ ​Support donor compliance requirements​
​●​ ​Preserve institutional knowledge​
​●​ ​Create a historical repository of programme evidence​
​●​ ​Enable learning from previous interventions​
​●​ ​Improve transparency and accountability​

​●​ ​Configure results framework.​
​●​ ​Create reporting forms.​
​●​ ​Develop dashboards.​
​●​ ​Configure notifications and workflows.​
​Phase 3: Pilot Testing​
​Activities:​
​●​ ​Pilot with selected departments.​
​●​ ​Collect user feedback.​
​●​ ​Refine system functionality.​
​●​ ​Develop user guidelines.​
​Phase 4: Organization-Wide Rollout​
​Activities:​
​●​ ​Train users.​
​●​ ​Upload departmental work plans.​
​●​ ​Launch the system.​

​9. Conclusion​
​The development of an Integrated Results-Based Programme Management Information System​
​will transform the organization's current reporting approach from an activity-focused process​
​into a strategic performance management system.​
​By linking work plans, implementation activities, outcomes, indicators, evidence, and reporting,​
​the system will enable the organization to measure not only what was done, but also the results​
​achieved and the changes created.​
​The RB-PMIS will strengthen accountability, improve reporting efficiency, enhance​
​organizational learning, and provide management with timely and reliable information to guide​
​strategic decisions and improve programme impact.​
