Background
The organization currently manages programme planning, implementation monitoring, reporting, evidence management and organizational learning through a combination of Excel workplans, Word reports, spreadsheets and manual document repositories.
While this approach has supported programme implementation, it presents several operational challenges, including:
Manual consolidation of departmental reports
Duplication of programme information across multiple documents
Delayed availability of management information
Limited linkage between strategic objectives and implementation activities
Difficulty tracking programme performance in real time
Fragmented storage of supporting evidence
Limited visibility of implementation progress
Inconsistent reporting quality
Time-consuming preparation of donor and management reports
Limited institutional knowledge retention
These limitations reduce organizational efficiency and affect timely decision-making.
Business Need
The organization requires an integrated digital platform that supports the complete programme management lifecycle rather than functioning solely as a reporting system.
The proposed Integrated Results-Based Programme Management Information System (RB-PMIS) will provide a centralized platform for planning, implementation, monitoring, evidence management, reporting, organizational learning and performance management.
The system will enable programme teams to manage implementation while automatically generating organizational reporting information from operational data entered throughout the implementation cycle.
Assumptions
Departments will adopt the system as the single source of record for workplans and progress reporting once rolled out.
Reliable internet connectivity and basic device access are available to programme staff who need to enter data.
Existing workplan and reporting templates can be reasonably mapped into a structured, database-backed format.
An organizational sponsor with the authority to enforce reporting compliance is identified before rollout.
Constraints
Budget and implementation timeline are to be confirmed during the BRD phase.
The system must align with existing donor compliance and data-protection requirements.
Any integration with existing finance, HR, or procurement systems is subject to those systems' technical constraints and is not assumed at this stage.
Vision
To establish an integrated digital programme management platform that transforms programme implementation, monitoring, reporting and organizational learning through real-time information, evidence-based decision-making and automated performance reporting.
Mission
To provide programme teams, management and stakeholders with reliable, timely and actionable programme information that supports improved programme performance, accountability and organizational learning.
Strategic Objectives
The RB-PMIS will:
Integrate planning, implementation, monitoring and reporting into a single platform.
Reduce manual reporting workload.
Improve programme accountability.
Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
Improve data quality.
Support adaptive programme management.
Improve organizational knowledge management.
Preserve institutional memory.
Strengthen donor compliance.
Improve programme performance.
Guiding Principles
The RB-PMIS will be designed around the following principles:
1. Data Entered Once, Used Many Times
Information should only be entered once. All reports, dashboards and analyses should automatically reuse existing data.
2. Planning Drives Reporting
Reports will originate from approved workplans. Departments will report progress against planned activities rather than creating independent reports.
3. Reporting is a By-product of Implementation
Programme staff should focus on implementing activities. Reporting should be automatically generated from implementation data.
4. Evidence-Based Management
Every reported achievement should be supported by verifiable evidence.
5. Results Before Activities
The system emphasizes outcomes and performance rather than simply documenting completed activities.
6. Continuous Learning
Lessons learned should become organizational knowledge that informs future programme design.
7. Real-Time Visibility
Management should have access to current implementation status at any time.
8. Accountability
Every activity, indicator and result should have a clearly assigned owner.

The RB-PMIS will manage the entire programme lifecycle:

Weekly Review Meetings   ↓
Monthly Reflection   ↓
Programme Reporting   ↓
Management Review   ↓
Lessons Learned   ↓
Knowledge Repository   ↓
Future Planning
In Scope
Strategic, annual, quarterly and monthly programme planning and workplan management
Activity assignment, tracking, and implementation status updates
Evidence upload, storage and linkage to activities and indicators
Weekly review and monthly reflection workflows
Automated programme and donor report generation from workplan and implementation data
Management dashboards and real-time (as defined in Success Criteria) implementation visibility
Lessons-learned capture and a searchable organizational knowledge repository
Role-based access control and data validation workflows
Out of Scope (for this phase)
Financial management, procurement, or payroll processing — the system may reference budget lines but will not replace finance systems
Human resources management, including staff appraisals and leave management
Direct beneficiary/case management systems, unless explicitly added during the BRD phase
Donor grant/contract management beyond report generation (e.g., full grants lifecycle management)
Public-facing external reporting portals, unless specifically requested during BRD scoping
Migration or historical data cleanup of pre-existing Excel/Word archives beyond what is agreed in the implementation plan
Items listed as out of scope may be revisited and formally brought into scope during the BRD phase if stakeholders agree.
